	\section{DCDC converter}
	\subsection{Input capacitor}
	I choose a ceramic input capacitor with the capacity $10uF$. I found the value recommended in the datasheet and I did not bother to calculate it. Hope it's good.
	
	\subsection{Minimum duty cycle}
	This is needed for the inductor calculation.
	
	\begin{equation}
		D_{min} = \frac{V_{OUT} + V_F}{V_{INMAX} - V_{SW}} = \frac{3.3 + 0.6}{12.6 - 0.6} = 0.31
	\end{equation}
	Where $V_F$ is the forward voltage of the diode and $V_{SW}$ is the voltage drop of the MOSFET inside the buck converter (I guess $0.3A\cdot{}200m\Omega$)
	
	\subsection{Inductor}
	I am choosing to have 20\% current ripple from the maximum current of 3A that the converter can supply. That is 0.6A.
	
	Then the minimum inductance is:
	\begin{equation}
		L_{MIN} = \frac{V_{OUT} + V_F}{\Delta{}I_{MAX}}\cdot\frac{1-D_{MIN}}{F_{SW}} = \frac{3.3+0.6}{0.6}\cdot\frac{1-0.31}{500 000} = 9uH
	\end{equation}
	
	\subsection{Output Capacitor}
	Once again I would just choose some value to have relatively low ESR with some 22-100uF capacity.
